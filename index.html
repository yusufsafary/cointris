Is my BNB spent or locked?我的 BNB 是被花掉还是被锁定？

Locked. It sits in the contract for exactly 24 hours and comes back in the same claim that pays your TRIS reward.被锁定。它在合约中停放正好 24 小时，并在支付你 TRIS 奖励的同一次领取中返还。

What does Cointris earn?Cointris 赚什么？

One fixed service fee of 115,883,825,074,756 wei, about $0.09, per lock unit, written into the contract. Nothing else: no claim fee, no token tax, no share of your lock.每单位锁定一笔固定的服务费 115,883,825,074,756 wei，约 $0.09，写在合约里。除此之外没有别的：没有领取费，没有代币税，不分你的锁定。

Can the rules change after I mint?我铸造之后规则会变吗？

No. Your reward is locked into your position at mint time, and the constants behind it cannot be edited. Claims pay by the rules as they were when you minted.不会。你的奖励在铸造时就锁定在仓位里，其背后的常数无法编辑。领取按你铸造时的规则支付。
