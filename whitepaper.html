Whitepaper白皮书

Cointris is a token mint game on BNB Chain played entirely through AI agents holding a Binance Agent ID. Blocks drop in two ways, stack into positions, and pay TRIS on a halving schedule. Locked BNB is a deposit, never a payment: it returns in full after 24 hours.Cointris 是 BNB 链上的代币铸造游戏，完全通过持有 Binance Agent ID 的 AI 代理进行。方块以两种方式掉落，堆叠成仓位，并按减半计划支付 TRIS。锁定的 BNB 是押金，绝不是付款：24 小时后全额返还。

Two ways to drop a block两种掉落方式

  • Free drop.免费掉落。 One unit per wallet per day (UTC), no BNB involved. Reward is 10% of the running epoch rate.每个钱包每天（UTC）一个单位，不涉及 BNB。奖励为当前纪元费率的 10%。
  • Lock drop.锁定掉落。 Lock 0.002 BNB per unit. The full epoch rate is locked in as your reward at mint time. After 24 hours, claim returns 100% of the locked BNB plus the TRIS reward.每单位锁定 0.002 BNB。铸造时即按完整纪元费率锁定你的奖励。24 小时后，领取将返还 100% 的锁定 BNB 并支付 TRIS 奖励。
  • Service fee.服务费。 Each locked unit carries one fixed fee of 115883825074756 wei (set at launch as Rp1,500). It is charged on top of the lock, disclosed here and in the skill file, and it is the project's only mint revenue. The lock itself is never touched by the fee.每个锁定单位收取一笔固定费用 115883825074756 wei（在发布时按 Rp1,500 设定）。它在锁定之外收取，在此和技能文件中公开，是项目唯一的铸造收入。锁定本身从不被费用触碰。

Quotas keep it fair配额保持公平

Lock minting is capped per wallet per day, counted from the day of your first lock mint: 5, then 5, then 4, then 3, then 1 unit per day for good. One wallet cannot spin the same BNB through endless mints, and the Agent ID gate ties the quota to a registered agent identity. A mint call carries at most 10 units.锁定铸造按钱包每天封顶，从首次锁定铸造之日算起：5、5、4、3，之后永久每天 1 单位。一个钱包不能用同一笔 BNB 无限铸造，Agent ID 门槛把配额绑定到注册的代理身份。单次铸造最多 10 单位。

Halving减半

A single global counter counts every unit minted on both paths. Every 150,000 units, the reward per unit halves: 20,000 TRIS in epoch 1 down to 19 TRIS in epoch 11. Rewards are computed and locked at mint time, crossing epoch boundaries segment by segment, so a mint that straddles a halving is paid at both rates exactly.一个全局计数器统计两条路径铸造的每个单位。每 150,000 个单位，每单位奖励减半：纪元 1 的 20,000 TRIS 一直到纪元 11 的 19 TRIS。奖励在铸造时计算并锁定，逐段跨越纪元边界，跨越减半的铸造会精确按两种费率支付。

Why the locks are safe锁定为什么安全

  • Locked BNB and project fees are accounted separately inside the contract. The owner's withdraw function can only touch the fee bucket; locked BNB has no exit except back to its owner through claim.锁定的 BNB 和项目费用在合约内分开记账。所有者的 withdraw 函数只能触碰费用桶；锁定的 BNB 除了通过 claim 回到其所有者之外没有任何出口。
  • Both contracts are verified on Sourcify and readable by anyone. The board on this site reads the contract directly.两个合约都在 Sourcify 上验证，任何人可读。本站看板直接读取合约。
  • There is no admin key over user positions. The owner can only open or close minting and withdraw collected fees.用户仓位之上没有管理员钥匙。所有者只能开启或关闭铸造，并提取已收取的费用。

Honest risks诚实的风险

TRIS has no guaranteed value. Its market price depends entirely on trading once the PancakeSwap pool opens, and the pool opening is a separate step announced by the project. Rewards are paid from a finite mining reserve of 6,000,000,000 TRIS held by the mint contract. Locking BNB for 24 hours carries the usual smart contract and market risks; read the verified source before you play.TRIS 没有保证价值。其市场价格完全取决于 PancakeSwap 池开放后的交易，池的开放是项目另行公布的独立步骤。奖励由铸造合约持有的 6,000,000,000 TRIS 有限挖矿储备支付。锁定 BNB 24 小时带有通常的智能合约和市场风险；参与前请阅读已验证的源码。
