Docs文档

Cointris is played by your agent, not by a web checkout. This site never asks you to connect a wallet. You give your Binance Agentic Wallet agent one of the prompts on the home page, and it calls the contract for you.Cointris 由你的代理游玩，而不是网页结账。本站从不要求你连接钱包。你把主页上的提示发给你的 Binance Agentic Wallet 代理，它会替你调用合约。

How a round works一轮如何进行

  • 1. Your wallet must hold a Binance Agent ID. No Agent ID, no mint, on either path.你的钱包必须持有 Binance Agent ID。没有 Agent ID，两条路径都不能铸造。
  • 2. Mint free once a day, and lock mint inside your daily quota. Every mint becomes a numbered position with its reward locked in.每天免费铸造一次，并在每日配额内锁定铸造。每次铸造都成为编号仓位，奖励随之锁定。
  • 3. Wait 24 hours per position. Positions mature independently.每个仓位等待 24 小时。仓位独立成熟。
  • 4. Claim matured positions in one call: locked BNB returns in full, TRIS rewards land in the same transaction.一次调用领取成熟仓位：锁定的 BNB 全额返还，TRIS 奖励在同一笔交易中到账。

FAQ问答

  • Is the locked BNB really returned in full?锁定的 BNB 真的全额返还吗？ Yes. The claim pays back exactly units x 0.002 BNB for every paid position. The only amount kept is the per unit service fee charged at mint time, disclosed before you mint.是的。领取对每个付费仓位精确返还 单位数 x 0.002 BNB。唯一保留的是铸造时收取的每单位服务费，铸造前已公开。
  • Why does a quota exist?为什么有配额？ Without it, one wallet could recycle the same BNB into unlimited rewards. Quotas tie minting speed to time, not to capital recycling.没有它，一个钱包可以用同一笔 BNB 循环获得无限奖励。配额把铸造速度绑定到时间，而不是资本循环。
  • Do unclaimed positions expire?未领取的仓位会过期吗？ Never. Matured positions wait until you claim them.永不过期。成熟仓位会一直等到你领取。
  • When does TRIS trade?TRIS 何时交易？ The PancakeSwap pool opens in a separate announced step using the initial pool allocation. Until then TRIS is a mint and hold token.PancakeSwap 池将使用初始池配额在一个单独公布的步骤中开放。在此之前 TRIS 是铸造和持有型代币。
  • My mint failed. Why?我的铸造失败了，为什么？ Usual causes: minting is not open yet, the wallet holds no Agent ID, the free drop was already taken today, the lock mint exceeds today's quota, or the value sent was not exactly units x 2115883825074756 wei. The skill file lists every cause for your agent.常见原因：铸造尚未开启、钱包没有 Agent ID、今天的免费掉落已领取、锁定铸造超出今日配额，或发送金额不是精确的 单位数 x 2115883825074756 wei。技能文件为你的代理列出了全部原因。
